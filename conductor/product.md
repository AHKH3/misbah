# Misbah (مصباح) — Product Definition
Status: Final / Authoritative  
Applies to: Entire Project (Product, Design, Engineering, Content)

---

## 1. What Misbah Is

Misbah is a **public, SEO-first directory** for **independent individual teachers** specializing in:
- Quran
- Tajweed
- Arabic language
- Related Islamic foundational studies

Misbah exists to **make discovery easier**, not to intermediate, manage, or optimize teaching outcomes.

The platform provides:
- A public teacher profile page
- A structured directory for browsing
- Direct contact visibility between student and teacher

Misbah provides **visibility only**.

---

## 2. What Misbah Is NOT (Non-Negotiable)

Misbah is NOT:

- An academy
- A marketplace
- A booking platform
- A payment processor
- An employer
- A guarantor of quality
- A ranking or recommendation engine

Misbah does NOT:
- Handle lessons
- Handle scheduling
- Handle student payments
- Assign students to teachers
- Endorse or certify teachers
- Compare teachers numerically or competitively

Any feature that implies the above is **forbidden**.

---

## 3. Target Users

### 3.1 Teachers (Primary User)
- Independent individuals
- Teaching privately (online or local)
- Often overlooked by large academies
- Seeking a minimal, professional public presence
- Not interested in “marketing tools” or dashboards

Misbah assumes teachers:
- Prefer clarity over promotion
- Prefer autonomy over systems
- Prefer ownership over dependency

### 3.2 Students / Parents (Secondary User)
- Searching intentionally (Google, AI, referrals)
- Want clarity, not persuasion
- Want to verify and decide themselves
- Often comparing *availability*, not popularity

Misbah does not “convince” them.
It **presents information clearly**.

---

## 4. Core Value Proposition

> “Misbah gives independent teachers a calm, trustworthy place to be found.”

- For teachers: visibility without loss of control  
- For students: discovery without manipulation  

No gamification.  
No pressure.  
No persuasion.

---

## 5. Teacher Eligibility Rules

Only **individual teachers** are allowed.

Explicitly forbidden:
- Academies
- Institutes
- Teams
- Multi-teacher brands
- Agencies

If an entity represents more than one teacher → **rejected**.

This rule is permanent and strategic.

---

## 6. Teacher Profile — Required Structure (MVP)

Each teacher profile MUST include:

### 6.1 Identity
- Full name (real name required)
- Country / location
- Teaching format (Online / Local)

### 6.2 Teaching Information
- Subjects taught (structured list)
- Target audience (e.g. Adults, Kids, Non-Arabs)
- Teaching format details (1-to-1, online tools)

### 6.3 Experience
- Text-based experience summary
- Qualifications as **user-declared text**
- No verification claims

### 6.4 Availability Status (Required)
Teacher must select **one**:
- Accepting new students
- Limited availability
- Not accepting students

This is informational only.
No automation.
No visibility boost.

### 6.5 Contact Method (Required)
At least one:
- WhatsApp
- Email
- Telegram
- External website

Misbah never intermediates contact.

---

## 7. Video Policy (Critical)

### 7.1 Required Video (Mandatory)
Each teacher MUST provide **one educational sample video**, such as:
- Quran recitation
- Lesson excerpt
- Teaching explanation
- Whiteboard explanation

Rules:
- Hosted externally (YouTube or similar)
- No upload to Misbah servers
- No editing or production quality requirements
- Purpose: show *actual teaching*, not self-promotion

### 7.2 Introductory Video (Optional)
- NOT required
- NOT encouraged
- Never mandatory

Rationale:
Misbah values **teaching evidence**, not self-marketing ability.

---

## 8. Manual Review & Activation

All profiles go through **human manual review**.

Review includes:
- Payment confirmation
- Scope check (individual teacher)
- Content clarity
- Video relevance (educational, not promotional)

Target review time:
- Within 24 hours

This process is positioned as:
> “Activation & Verification of Submission”
Not “approval for acceptance”.

---

## 9. Pricing Philosophy (Product-Level)

Misbah pricing is:
- Low
- Flat
- Predictable

Default strategy:
- Annual subscription
- First year discount
- Renewal at full price

Pricing is **for visibility**, not for leads, students, or success.

---

## 10. Discovery Model

Misbah relies on:
- Search engines (SEO)
- AI discovery (LLMs, assistants)
- Direct sharing (links)

Misbah does NOT provide:
- Internal ranking
- “Best” lists
- Algorithmic promotion
- Engagement metrics

Discovery is **external-first**.

---

## 11. Forbidden Features (Permanent)

The following are explicitly forbidden:

- Teacher ratings or reviews
- Student counts
- Earnings displays
- Popularity indicators
- “Top teachers”
- Featured promotions
- Algorithmic sorting by performance
- Testimonials written by the platform

Any feature that pressures teachers to “perform” socially is rejected.

---

## 12. Legal & Responsibility Boundaries

- Teachers are independent.
- All information is user-declared.
- Misbah does not verify credentials.
- Students are responsible for their decisions.
- All teaching agreements happen off-platform.

These boundaries must be visible in UI copy.

---

## 13. Product North Star

Misbah succeeds if:
- A serious parent finds a suitable teacher calmly
- A teacher feels respected, not exploited
- The platform stays boring, stable, and trusted

Misbah is not built to scale fast.
It is built to **last quietly**.
