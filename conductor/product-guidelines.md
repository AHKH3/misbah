# Product Guidelines: Misbah (مصباح)

## 1. Voice and Tone
**Misbah's voice is Calm, Minimalist, and Factual.**

The platform's copy must strictly adhere to these principles:
- **Neutral & Factual:** Describe services and features without hyperbole or emotional language.
- **Concise:** Use the minimum number of words necessary to convey information. Avoid fluff and filler.
- **Non-Promotional:** Avoid "salesy" language. Misbah is a tool for discovery, not a marketing engine.
- **Clarifying:** Always reinforce what Misbah is (a directory) and what it is not (a school/marketplace).

### Terminology Rules
- **DO NOT USE:** "Hire," "Book," "Our teachers," "Best tutors," "Verified," "Guaranteed."
- **DO USE:** "Listing," "Directory," "Connect directly," "Independent teachers," "User-declared information."

---

## 2. Visual Identity & UI Principles
The interface should feel "invisible," prioritizing content readability and system performance.

- **Typography-First:** High-contrast text (Black on White) using **System Fonts** only (no external font loading).
- **Generous Whitespace:** Prioritize large margins and padding to create a "Calm" and uncluttered layout.
- **Monochrome with Single Accent:** The palette is primarily Black, White, and Gray. A single accent color (e.g., Deep Green) is used exclusively for primary calls to action.
- **No Decorative Imagery:** Zero use of stock photography, decorative illustrations, or heavy animations. Icons should be functional and minimal.
- **Tactile Depth:** Subtle, multi-layered drop shadows for cards to create a sense of structure without relying on borders or colors.

---

## 3. Brand Messaging & Positioning
**Core Message:** "Misbah provides visibility. Discovery is the service."

### Legal and Religious Neutrality
- **The "Discovery Only" Mandate:** All brand messaging must focus strictly on the ease of finding teachers.
- **Zero Endorsement:** Misbah never assesses, ranks, or endorses teachers. The platform is an empty vessel for user-submitted information.
- **Responsibility Transfer:** Messaging must clearly signal that all interactions, vetting, and payments happen outside of Misbah's jurisdiction.

---

## 4. Operational Principles
- **SEO as UX:** Every page design decision is secondary to how that page will be parsed by search engines and LLMs.
- **Privacy by Default:** No visitor tracking, no accounts for students, and minimal data collection for teachers.
- **Manual Verification:** Payment and profile activation is a human-gatekept process to ensure scope and quality (not credentials).
